The two-minute version

  1. Race: Human on Alliance. On Horde, Undead is the comfiest to level and Orc is best for raids and demons.
  2. Level as Affliction. Your demon tanks, your DoTs tick, and Drain Life heals you, so you rarely stop to rest.
  3. Your wand is your best friend early. Put DoTs up, then wand instead of spending mana. Upgrade your wand before your armor.
  4. Life Tap while you walk so you reach every pull with full mana, then heal back with Drain Life.
  5. Get every demon from its class quest: Voidwalker at 10, Succubus or Incubus at 20, Felhunter at 30.
  6. At 40 you get a free mount, Dual Spec and your capstone talent (Wrack, Demonic Pact or Incinerate).
  7. At 60 pick your raid spec, work through the pre-raid list and start the Dreadsteed quest chain.
Re-drag spells after every trainer visit. A spell on your action bar doesn't jump to the new rank when you buy it. Drag it from the spellbook again.

Step 2 · Know your tools

What Forever changed about the Warlock

  • Banes are new. Bane of Agony and Bane of Doom are no longer Curses, so you can keep a Bane and a utility Curse on the same target.
  • DoTs can crit. That's what talents like Pandemic and Malevolence build on.
  • Demons scale with your stats and gear.
  • Life Tap scales with Spirit: at max rank, 430 mana plus 1 mana per Spirit (1.2 with 2/2 Improved Life Tap).
  • Drain Soul has a growing chance per tick to give a Soul Shard. It was cut on Nov 3, so getting the kill during the channel is the reliable way.
  • Firestone and Spellstone are now weapon imbues that stack with wizard oils. Firestone gives Fire damage and crit; Spellstone gives Shadow damage and spell haste.
  • Curse of the Elements now boosts all magic damage taken. Curse of Shadows is gone.
  • Incubus joins at level 20 as an alternative to the Succubus. Demonic Sacrifice now lasts 2 hours.
  • Fear now has 20 yards of range.
  • Wands no longer gain damage from spell power, and you must stand still for 0.5 sec before a wand fires.
  • Ritual of Summoning (level 20) is the only way to summon players to dungeons.

Rotation by level
  1. 1–4: Shadow Bolt, then Immolate while the bolt is in the air so both land together. Finish in melee.
  2. 4–8: Corruption and Immolate, then wand.
  3. 8–10: Bane of Agony and wand. It ramps slowly, so your demon keeps the mob's attention.
  4. 10+: Bane and Corruption from range, Immolate as the mob closes, then wand or Drain Life. Take two mobs at once with the Voidwalker tanking one.
  5. Fear juggling: Fear a second mob away while you finish the first. Fear reaches 20 yards.
Mana and survival
  • Life Tap while walking between mobs, so you reach every pull with full mana.
  • Heal back with Drain Life, Siphon Life and Demon Skin.
  • Don't reapply Corruption before it runs out. It ticks every 3 sec.
  • Channel Drain Soul on a mob your demon is tanking to farm Soul Shards.
  • Set a focus target to keep two mobs dotted.
  • Stats: wand DPS first early on, then spell power, then Stamina, Intellect and Spirit.

Casts Shadow Bolt and sends your demon at the target. Hold Ctrl to call your demon back instead.

Your checklist at 60
  1. Start the Summon Dreadsteed quest chain for your epic mount.
  2. Respec into your raid spec with Dual Spec, and keep Affliction as the second spec for farming.
  3. Farm reputation: Theramore Expeditionary Force or Darkspear Raiders for shoulders, weapon and off-hand, and The Watchers for your signet ring.
  4. Level Tailoring and craft the Felcloth pieces.
  5. Run Blackmaw Hold, Shaper's Terrace, Blackrock Spire, Dire Maul and Scholomance.
  6. In raids: hand out Healthstones, put a Soulstone on a healer, agree on Curses with the other Warlocks, and summon people with Ritual of Summoning.

Bonus

PvP and duels

Why Warlock is strong
  • LFCarry rates Warlock S tier in PvP. Warlocks took 1st and 2nd in the level 30 beta duel finals and won 79% of finals duels.
  • Lots of control: Fear (20 yd), Howl of Terror, Death Coil, Seduction, Curse of Exhaustion.
  • The Felhunter's Spell Lock and Devour Magic shut down casters.
  • Drains and Healthstones keep you alive.
How you lose
  • Low mobility, cloth armor, and damage pushes back your casts.
  • DoTs need time. A burst class that reaches you first can win before they ramp.
  • Managing your demon and its commands takes practice.
Duel game plan
  1. Bring the Felhunter against casters and set it on them, with Spell Lock on a mouseover macro.
  2. Open with your Curse and DoTs from max range, then Fear so they tick for free.
  3. When they break Fear and reach you, Death Coil or Howl of Terror to reset the distance.
  4. Drain Life and Healthstone to win long fights.
  5. Racials: Human Will to Survive breaks stuns, Undead Will of the Forsaken breaks Fear, and Orc Shatter Curse removes Curses.

Getting started

Ruleset: Normal, PvP, Roleplay or Hardcore?Normal (PvE) is what about 90% of players pick.
  • Forever has no separate servers. Everyone in a region shares one world, and you pick a ruleset instead.
  • Normal is PvE. PvP lets the other faction attack you in the world. Roleplay is for people who play in character.
  • Hardcore: if you die, the character isn't deleted. It loses its Hardcore tag and becomes a Normal character, so there's no harm trying it.
  • Hardcore has far fewer players, so dungeon groups and help with elite quests are harder to find.
Faction: Alliance or Horde?Pick the same faction as your friends. You can't group across factions.
  • Alliance and Horde players can't group together, so match your friends.
  • You can make characters on the other faction later, unless you have a PvP character: a PvP Alliance character stops you from making Horde characters.
  • Every class can now be played on either faction, for example the Undead Paladin or the Dwarf Shaman.
Race, class and roleRace barely changes damage. It decides your starting zone and your class options.
  • Your race decides which classes you can play and where you start.
  • Racials are situational, not big damage boosts: Dwarf Stoneform makes you immune to bleeds, poisons and diseases, and Human Will to Survive removes stuns. Treat it as mostly a cosmetic choice; Blizzard keeps adjusting these.
  • Tank leads the group. Forever has 9 brand-new dungeons that nobody knows yet, which makes this a good time to learn tanking. Healer keeps the group alive. Both are more stressful than ranged DPS.
  • Playing with a friend of another race? Not every starting zone has every class trainer. A Warlock who starts in a Night Elf zone won't find a Warlock trainer, and you train every 2 levels.
Legacy system: what to pickWell-Rested plus Talented gives you talent points from level 5.
  • Legacy points fill a reward track that unlocks things like a pet and a mount.
  • You earn points from Legacy Challenges: a class reaching 25, 45 and 60 (3 points per class, 27 for all nine), plus professions, PvP, exploring and dungeons.
  • Three Legacy trees: Professions (faster leveling, more rare materials), Adventure and Resourcefulness (longer food buffs, less durability loss on death).
  • Best pick per the video: 5 points in Well-Rested, then 5 in Talented. You start getting talent points at level 5 instead of 10, so you have 5 extra while leveling.
  • This system is new and may be rebalanced. Check the current tooltips in game.

Auto-attack keeps turning offAuto-attack is a toggle. A macro can make the button only turn it on.

Pressing auto-attack twice turns it off, and you end up standing there getting hit. A macro fixes this so the button always turns it on. The command for that is /startattack. Warlocks mostly fight with a wand: /cast !Shoot does the same job, so pressing it again never stops your wand.

Quests & leveling

What do the ! and ? marks mean?Yellow ! = take it. Silver ? = not done. Silver ! = too low level. Blue = repeatable.

If a quest offers a choice of rewards, you have to click one before the game lets you turn it in.

Where do I go for this quest?Press M. Quest areas are marked on the map.
  • Press M: your accepted quests show where to go and where to turn in.
  • Click an enemy: under its name (bottom right) you'll see if it's for a quest, like "0/10". In a group it shows your friends' progress too.
  • Group quest items are now shared out evenly, so you and a friend both reach 10/10 at about the same time.
  • Your quest log holds 40 quests (it used to be 20). Untrack quests to tidy the screen, but don't forget them.
  • Elite quests ask you to kill an elite. Don't solo them; find a group in the area.
The fastest ways to levelQuesting, plus dungeon quests. Grinding is rarely needed now.
  • Questing is the main way. Most of the XP comes from turning the quest in.
  • Dungeons: most of their XP now comes from the dungeon's quests, so you don't need to run the same dungeon over and over. Run it again only for a gear drop you missed.
  • Grinding mobs is rarely needed: Forever added over 1,000 new quests, so each quest hub leads to the next.
Rested XP: the blue part of my XP barDouble XP until the marker. Log out in an inn or a capital city.
Class trainer: new spells every 2 levelsNew ranks are NOT put on your bar for you. Re-drag them.
  • Visit your trainer at levels 2, 4, 6, 8 and so on. It gets more expensive as you level, so save gold for it.
  • The most common new-player mistake: new ranks go into your spellbook, not onto your bar. Open the spellbook (P). A glowing spell isn't on your bar yet. Drag it over the old rank.
  • Need an old cheap rank on purpose? Use the arrow in the spellbook and pick "show all spell ranks".
Talents and respecsTalents start at 10. Respec at a trainer, but not every trainer can do it.
  • Your talent tree decides your role. A Warlock can be Affliction, Demonology or Destruction. All three deal damage.
  • Talents can give new abilities (like Wrack) or change how one works. New talent spells also glow in the spellbook until you put them on your bar.
  • Most builds spend points in more than one tree. Name your main one when people ask: "I'm an Affliction Warlock", not just "a Warlock".
  • To reset talents, talk to a trainer and choose to unlearn them. Not every trainer offers this, so look up the nearest one that does.

Combat & death

Can I fight this mob? Read its colorsYellow or green level = fine. Orange = hard. Red or skull = walk away.
Why am I losing fights?Fighting 2 mobs at once, or mobs 3+ levels above you.
  • Two mobs standing together? Pull or crowd-control one first. Classic-style combat is punishing.
  • Use your self-buffs and crowd control (Warlock: Fear), then bandage if a fight goes bad.
  • Losing? Stop attacking and keep running. A mob stops chasing (leashes) once you stop hitting it, unless you pull more on the way.
  • Mobs hitting your back can daze you and slow you. Try to keep facing them as you back off, or stun and run.
  • Let mobs hit you sometimes: your defense skill levels from being hit and makes you harder to hit.
How do I heal up?Food out of combat. Potions and bandages in combat.
  • Food restores health and drinks restore mana, out of combat only. Some food gives a Well Fed buff (example: +7 Stamina and Spirit for 15 min).
  • Potions work in combat. Healing potions are now made with First Aid, not Alchemy. Buying them on the auction house is fine too.
  • Bandages work in combat but are a channeled heal over time (example: 114 over 6 sec). Get distance first, because a hit cancels the rest. They have a cooldown.
  • Warlock: Drain Life and Healthstones heal you too.
I died. Now what?Run your ghost to your body for free, or use the Spirit Healer for a price.
  • Resurrection sickness cuts your attributes, damage, armor and resistances by 75%. You can barely kill anything until it ends.
  • The Spirit Healer is often still faster than a long corpse run: by the time you repair, turn in quests and walk to the next mobs, the sickness is nearly gone.
  • Repair whenever you pass a repair vendor ("Repair all items"). Broken gear acts like an empty slot. The armor figure on screen turns yellow, then red, as pieces wear out.
  • Trick: dying near a graveyard next to a quest town can save you a long walk back.
PvP honor: don't kill grey playersKilling much lower players is dishonorable and lowers your rank.
  • Players who are green or higher to you count as honorable kills.
  • Grey (much lower level) players are dishonorable kills, which pull your honor rank down.
  • Your honor tab tracks your rank and your kills this week, last week and lifetime.

Gear & stats

Gear slots, and when you'll find themShoulders and rings from about 15, neck early 20s, helmet late 20s, trinkets 30s.
  • Slots: head, neck, shoulders, back (cloak), chest, shirt (cosmetic), tabard (guild), wrists, hands, waist, legs, feet, 2 rings, 2 trinkets, main hand, off hand and ranged.
  • Bows and guns need ammo in the ammo slot. Wands don't.
  • Rough timing: shoulders and rings from about level 15, neck in your early 20s, helmet in your late 20s, trinkets in your 30s. Crafted items can come earlier.
Armor typesWear your heaviest type and everything lighter.
Item colors: what's good?Grey < white < green < blue < purple.
Soulbound, bind on equip, bind on pickupSoulbound items can only go to a vendor. Everything else can be sold or mailed.
  • Bind on pickup: it's yours the moment you loot it.
  • Bind on equip: tradeable until you put it on.
  • Soulbound: can't be traded or sold on the auction house, not even to your own alts. Sell it to a vendor if you don't need it.
  • Found a cool unbound item for another class? Mail it to your alt for later.
Weapon skills and Weapon MastersEach weapon type levels as you hit with it. New types are learned from a Weapon Master.
  • Check your weapon skill levels in the character menu (C), under Skills.
  • Each hit has a chance to raise that weapon's skill by 1. A low skill means you miss a lot against mobs your level.
  • Level a new weapon type early. If you wait, you may need to go to a low-level area just to swing at easy mobs.
  • "You do not have the required proficiency"? Visit a Weapon Master. Example from the video: daggers cost 10 silver to learn.
  • Ask any Weapon Master "What can other weapon masters teach?" and they'll tell you who teaches what, and where.
  • Horde examples from the video: Sayoc in Thunder Bluff (maces, staves, guns), Hanashi next to him (bows, axes, staves, thrown) and Archibald in the Undercity War Quarter (crossbows, daggers, swords, polearms).

Bags & gold

My bags are always fullBuy cheap bags early, sell junk in one click, and use an auction alt.
  • Bags first. Early 8-slot bags can cost just a few silver on the auction house (Containers → Bags). Upgrade to 10, 12, 14 and 16 slots later.
  • Items with a little gold coin icon are junk. Click Sell Junk at any vendor to sell them all.
  • Each character has its own bank, so a bank alt gives you free extra storage. Buying more bank slots gets expensive fast; save that gold for your mount.
Selling on the auction houseSell tab, right-click the item, and it auto-prices at the lowest listing.
  1. Auction houses are only in the main cities.
  2. Open the Sell tab and right-click an item in your bags. It fills in the current cheapest price for you, so you don't need to undercut by hand.
  3. Click Auction.
  4. Only list items that sell for more than the vendor pays. With Auctionator, the tooltip shows both prices (example from the video: 13 silver at the vendor vs 42 silver on the auction house).
Can't find the bank, trainer or weapon master?Talk to a city guard. It marks the spot on your map.

Talk to any guard in town and pick what you're looking for (bank, weapon master and so on). A flag appears on your map. Turn on minimap tracking (magnifying glass) to see the auction house, bank, innkeeper and mailbox.

Groups & dungeons

Finding a groupPress I. List yourself, or browse and invite.
  • Press I. To join: Create Listing, pick your role, the dungeon and a playstyle (Relaxed, or Learning if you're new), then List Self.
  • To build a group: open the Group Browser. Healers and tanks are the hardest to find, so invite them quickly. Hit Search again now and then to keep the list fresh.
  • Mix armor and weapon types in your group so every drop has someone to use it.
  • Questing: "kill 5 of these" goes twice as fast in a group, and everyone gets credit.
Dungeons: when and howStart around 15. Go a couple of levels above the low end, and grab every dungeon quest first.
  • Start around level 15. There's a dungeon every few levels up to 60, plus 9 new ones in Forever.
  • New to a dungeon? Don't go at the bottom of its range. For a level 25 dungeon, go at about 27.
  • Pick up the dungeon's quests first (usually 3–5, sometimes spread around the world). Turning them in can give a whole level.
  • Not sure which dungeons fit your level? Ask any innkeeper "Tell me about the dungeons I could explore".
  • Before raiding at 60, collect your pre-raid best-in-slot gear so you can pull your weight.
Right-click someone's portraitFocus, inspect, trade, whisper, follow, view talents.

Right-click a player's portrait to set them as focus (used by focus macros), promote to leader, whisper, inspect gear, view talents, trade, or follow. Tell people before you auto-follow them so they don't lose you on a corner.

Professions & reputation

How professions work2 primary plus all 3 secondary. Recipe color = skill-up chance.
  • Learn from a profession trainer before you can gather or craft. Each profession has 4 ranks: Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert and Artisan. Go back to the trainer at each cap (for example, 150).
  • An early starter-zone quest lets you pick Mining, Skinning or Herbalism. Use the reward item to unlock it.
  • Tools: Mining needs a pickaxe and Skinning needs a skinning knife, both from vendors.
  • Level gathering as you go. If you skip nodes, they turn red in higher zones and you'll have to go back.
  • Mining with a friend: each of you hits the same node once, and each gets a chance to skill up from it.
  • You can abandon a profession anytime, but you lose all its progress.

Travel & mounts

Getting around fasterFlight paths, hearthstone, zeppelins, mage portals, or even dying.
  • Flight paths: talk to every flight master (green !) in every new town to unlock it. Flights are cheap. You'll forget at least once.
  • Hearthstone: set your home at an innkeeper and teleport back there. It has a 1-hour cooldown. Re-bind it each time you move to a new zone.
  • Zeppelins: free rides between Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms, from big towers outside major towns.
  • Mage portals: to cities, for example at the end of a dungeon. The Mage must step through last, or the portal closes.
  • Dying on purpose: if the graveyard sits next to your quest town, it can save a long walk.
Mounts: cost and speedWarlock: your level 40 mount is free from a class quest.
  • Buy from your race's riding instructor. In Forever, buying the riding skill now gives you the mount too: one purchase.
  • Have about 80–90 gold ready as you near 40, so you can train the moment you can. (As a Warlock, you can save that for the epic mount instead.)
  • Like another race's mount? Get Honored with that faction to buy it.
