# Eldorado's Price Floor Swallows Wholesale Margins

No supplier can be proven cheapest from public data, and for most products it would not matter: on 11 November 2026, Eldorado's lowest prices already sat at or below what legitimate wholesale supply costs. Every wholesaler checked, FazerCards included, hides per-product prices behind a login, and the visible discounts are small — **2.5–3.55% off Steam top-ups** at FazerCards (behind a $9.99–$29.99 monthly plan), **9% off PUBG UC** at SEAGM's public store, and **6% off face value** in GamesDrop's own example. Eldorado sellers, meanwhile, price Genshin, Mobile Legends, Valorant, Fortnite and Robux **roughly 22–65% under official prices**, so a reseller buying from a legitimate supplier loses money on each of those sales. Profit is possible in only two places: PUBG Mobile UC, if a reseller tier gets 660 UC down to about **$8 (19% under official)**, and USD gift cards that sell above face — **Steam $50–$100 and PlayStation $20** — where buyer demand is unproven. Both sit behind entry gates: **250 completed sales at 98%+ feedback** for top-ups, and manual approval for gift cards. The risk is one-sided: a supplier cheap enough to beat the market is the one most likely to sell stolen or rule-breaking stock, which Eldorado punishes with **permanent suspension on the first offense** — on the single seller account your boosting business also uses. Treat FazerCards as a free five-day price check, not a place to keep money, and decide with a small, capped test that has hard go and stop numbers.

## Every supplier hides its prices, and verified discounts stop near 10%

Not one wholesaler researched shows its per-product prices to the public. FazerCards says "the actual numbers live on each plan's checkout page" ([FazerCards pricing](https://reseller.fazercards.com/en/pricing)), EZ Cards unlocks pricing only "once you're approved" ([EZ Cards](https://ezcards.io/)), and PrepaidForge lists monthly spending tiers from €500 to €9,500 with no percentages ([PrepaidForge](https://www.prepaidforge.com/)). The hard numbers that do exist are small. FazerCards' only published discount is **2.5% (Bronze), 3% (Silver) or 3.55% (Gold) on Steam top-ups** ([FazerCards](https://reseller.fazercards.com)). SEAGM's public store sells **PUBG Mobile 660 UC for $9.09 against the official $9.99**, the best verified price for that pack ([SEAGM](https://seagm.com/pubg-mobile-uc-top-up-global); [Buffget](https://buffget.com/news/pubg-mobile-uc-top-up-prices-6-packs-cost-per-uc-2026)), and MooGold sells it as a code for $9.30 ([MooGold](https://moogold.com/product/pubg-mobile/)). GamesDrop's fee sheet works through an example of **$100 of face value bought for $94** ([GamesDrop fee sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15PFi4wMBYCUQDxGBkqEqcdZeRQP2do1H/edit)). Bigger discounts appear only as "up to" promises. Lapakgaming's Master reseller tier offers **up to 19% off after a deposit of IDR 10,000,000 (about $553)** ([Joytify reseller page](https://www.joytify.com/es-mx/reseller)); Indonesia's VCGamers offers up to 23% but requires an Indonesian national ID and tax number ([VCGamers](https://www.vcgamers.com/about/h2h/)); and LikeCard says its merchants "keep up to 25% commission per sale" ([Money20/20 listing](https://app.money2020middleeast.com/event/money20-20-middle-east-2026/product/UHJvZHVjdF8xNTI0NzEx)). The largest margin figures circulating online — 15–25% on PUBG UC, 10–18% on PlayStation — come from Marix, a content site that sends readers to one supplier and shows no method or dates ([Marix](https://marix.app/library/guides/wholesale-digital-goods-2026)). Treat them as advertising.

### FazerCards has a wide catalog, hidden prices and no named company

FazerCards is a working B2B platform with a strong catalog: **10,000+ products across 700+ games**, including Player-ID top-ups for PUBG, Free Fire, Mobile Legends, Genshin and Valorant, gift cards for Steam, PlayStation, Xbox, Google Play and Apple, and Robux "including GamePass-based offers" ([FazerCards](https://reseller.fazercards.com)). Every plan includes the REST API and webhooks, and the **free five-day Gold trial needs no card** ([FazerCards pricing](https://reseller.fazercards.com/en/pricing)). Funding is by Binance Pay or USDT on TRC20, BEP20, TON or Aptos ([FazerCards Terms](https://reseller.fazercards.com/en/terms)), which works from Egypt without card limits. The weak points are trust and protection when something goes wrong. The Terms name **no company, registered address or country** — only "the operator of FazerCards Reseller" — and support runs by email and Telegram from 08:00 to 20:00 Moscow time ([FazerCards contacts](https://reseller.fazercards.com/en/contacts)). The Terms, last updated 2 July 2026, say **nothing about invalid or revoked codes**; delivered orders are "final and non-refundable"; and the operator can reverse "improper credits" and debit your balance ([FazerCards Terms](https://reseller.fazercards.com/en/terms)). FazerCards says it "aggregates dozens of suppliers" and claims no publisher authorization ([FazerCards](https://reseller.fazercards.com)). Its Trustpilot page holds just **6 reviews averaging 3.0**, including "Support not responding at all" and orders left unfulfilled "for days" ([Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/reseller.fazercards.com)). The subscription also eats thin margins: at the published Steam rate, the Bronze fee of $9.99 cancels the entire rebate on the first ~$400 of monthly volume, and Gold needs about $845 a month just to pay for itself. Finally, the Terms allow sales to end customers through "your own sites, bots, stores, and apps" but never mention marketplaces, so get written confirmation that Eldorado resale is allowed ([FazerCards Terms](https://reseller.fazercards.com/en/terms)).

## Zero commission still leaves a 5% cut and two entry gates

Eldorado's fee table looks generous. Gift cards carry **0% seller commission "for all games"**, and top-ups are 0% as standard, with named exceptions: 15% for all Roblox games; 10% for Amazon, ChatGPT, Crunchyroll and Spotify; 5% for Fortnite, EA FC, Forza Horizon 6, Pokémon Go and Roblox; 4% for Genshin; 3% for Apex; and 2% for Valorant. In-game currency is 5%, or 15% for Roblox ([Eldorado Fees](https://intercom.help/eldoradogg/en/articles/8409025-fees)). Your real cost is the withdrawal. **Skrill takes 5% + $1 and pays in euros converted from dollars**; Payoneer takes 4% + $2 and pays in dollars, with a $50 minimum in most countries; SEPA is 4% + $5; Bitcoin and USDC are 6% + $20 ([Eldorado Withdrawals](https://intercom.help/eldoradogg/en/articles/8409026-withdrawals)). A $100 gift-card sale therefore leaves about $94. Because you withdraw through Skrill and then into Egyptian pounds, every dollar is converted twice; for any withdrawal above $100, Payoneer's 4% + $2 costs less than Skrill's 5% + $1 ($42 against $51 on $1,000), though the cost of moving Payoneer dollars into an Egyptian bank was not researched. Other marketplaces are worse for this category: G2G charges a fixed 4.99% on top-ups and gift cards ([G2G](https://support.g2g.com/support/solutions/articles/5000001417)), and Z2U charges 5–9% plus an add-on fee to the buyer ([Z2U](https://www.z2u.com/faq/what-are-the-fees-to-sell.html)). If the margin fails on Eldorado, moving to another marketplace will not save it.

The buyer pays an extra **8% + $0.30** at checkout ([Eldorado Fees](https://intercom.help/eldoradogg/en/articles/8409025-fees)), and that caps your price. A card-paying buyer of 660 UC at Eldorado's lowest $9.50 pays $10.56 — more than PUBG's own $9.99 ([Eldorado PUBG](https://www.eldorado.gg/pubg-mobile-uc-top-up/t/21)). Only buyers who pay from their Eldorado balance avoid the fee ([Dispute process](https://intercom.help/eldoradogg/en/articles/8409016-order-dispute-process)), so for most products there is no room to price above the official store.

Two gates come before the first sale. Every seller passes ID checks: 18 or older, a document such as a passport, and a selfie ([Eldorado ID verification](https://intercom.help/eldoradogg/en/articles/8409001-why-do-i-need-to-verify-my-id-to-sell)). **Top-ups require at least 250 completed sales, a feedback score of 98% or higher, and a separate application** ([Top-up guidelines](https://support.eldorado.gg/en/articles/12032614-top-up-and-subscription-seller-guidelines)). **Gift cards are open only to "approved sellers"**, with no published criteria ([Gift card guidelines](https://support.eldorado.gg/en/articles/11703747-gift-card-seller-guidelines)). The gift-card category covers 17 brands, from Steam and PlayStation to PUBG UC, Razer Gold and Roblox, and accepts only codes or redeem links ([Eldorado](https://www.eldorado.gg/); [Gift card guidelines](https://support.eldorado.gg/en/articles/11703747-gift-card-seller-guidelines)), which makes it the natural home for codes bought from a distributor. Eldorado allows one account per seller ([Seller rules](https://intercom.help/eldoradogg/en/articles/8408997-seller-rules)), so all of this has to be built on your boosting account. The top-up rule counts "completed sales" without naming a category, so boosting orders should count — confirm with support before planning around it.

Money also comes back slowly. After you mark an order delivered, the buyer has up to 3 days to confirm; Eldorado then holds the funds for **4 days on gift cards, 7 on Roblox currency, and 5 on "other categories"** — or no hold for "established sellers" with 200+ orders, $3,000+ in sales and cancellations of 15% or less over 180 days — plus up to 12 hours of processing ([TradeShield](https://intercom.help/eldoradogg/en/articles/8409006-tradeshield-selling)). In the worst case a gift-card dollar returns in about 7.5 days and a top-up dollar in about 8.5, so roughly one and a half weeks of sales stay locked up, on top of your prepaid balance at the supplier. If the boosting account already counts as established, top-up payouts should skip the 5-day hold, since top-ups are not named in the hold table.

## The cheapest supply is the supply that gets accounts banned

Eldorado's rules leave no room for doubtful stock. Gift cards must be "accurate, legitimate, and legally obtained", and selling from "illegal, stolen, or restricted sources" can bring permanent suspension ([Gift card guidelines](https://support.eldorado.gg/en/articles/11703747-gift-card-seller-guidelines)). For top-ups, "topping up from unofficial or unauthorized sources" and paying with stolen cards mean **permanent suspension on the first offense**, and every top-up must come from "the game's or platform's official channels" ([Top-up guidelines](https://support.eldorado.gg/en/articles/12032614-top-up-and-subscription-seller-guidelines)). Eldorado can also hold your balance for up to 30 days to refund buyers ([Seller rules](https://intercom.help/eldoradogg/en/articles/8408997-seller-rules)). The rules never say whether a third-party wholesaler counts as an "official channel", and PUBG's own notice says it "never authorizes any individual or third-party platform to perform intermediary purchases" ([Buffget, quoting PUBG Mobile](https://buffget.com/news/pubg-mobile-uc-refund-chargeback-what-happens-official-rules-2026)). If Eldorado questions where your stock came from, a supplier that names no company and claims no authorization — FazerCards — gives you the weakest evidence. And because Eldorado allows only one account per seller, the account at stake is the one your boosting business runs on.

Bad codes land on you, not on the supplier. Eldorado refunds buyers when "the code is invalid, or the code was already redeemed or expired" ([Refund policy](https://intercom.help/eldoradogg/en/articles/8409020-refund-policy)), gives you 12 hours to answer a dispute ([Seller rules](https://intercom.help/eldoradogg/en/articles/8408997-seller-rules)) and 120 hours to resolve a gift-card complaint, and can ask you to prove a code's status with a balance checker or the issuer ([Gift card guidelines](https://support.eldorado.gg/en/articles/11703747-gift-card-seller-guidelines)). Suppliers rarely cover that loss. FazerCards treats delivered orders as final ([FazerCards](https://reseller.fazercards.com)); an EZ PIN customer says about $6,000 of expired PUBG UC vouchers were never refunded ([Trustpilot EZ PIN](https://dk.trustpilot.com/review/ezpin.com)); LikeCard reviewers report refusals under a no-refund policy ([Trustpilot LikeCard](https://nz.trustpilot.com/review/likecard.com)); a PrepaidForge buyer got only part of a bad PlayStation batch refunded, after sending video evidence, in July 2026 ([Trustpilot PrepaidForge](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/prepaidforge.com)); and MooGold refunds only to its own wallet ([Trustpilot MooGold](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/moogold.com)). The arithmetic is harsh: **at a 5% margin, one dead $10 code wipes out the profit from 20 sales**. Eldorado's TradeShield covers payment disputes during its protection window, but Eldorado publishes nothing on who pays when a code is revoked after your funds are released ([TradeShield](https://intercom.help/eldoradogg/en/articles/8409006-tradeshield-selling)).

Publishers are enforcing, and the buyer's account takes the hit first. In September 2026 Xbox banned accounts that redeemed fraudulent codes "purchased through reseller channels", and its CTO told players to buy only from authorized retailers ([Pure Xbox](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/09/xbox-cto-warns-against-the-dangers-of-buying-fraudulent-game-codes)). Valve is pulling physical Steam cards from shops because scammers "continued abusing gift cards despite years of mitigation efforts" ([Bitdefender](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/blog/hotforsecurity/scammers-steam-gift-cards-dead)). Criminals use hijacked accounts to buy gift codes that "can be resold on third-party marketplaces, often for cryptocurrency" ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/cybersecurity/2026/gift-card-loophole-gives-hackers-a-new-way-to-cash-out/)). Roblox bans "using third-party services to buy, sell, trade, or give away Robux" while exempting gift cards ([Roblox rules](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313410-Roblox-Rules-of-Conduct)), which puts FazerCards' GamePass-based Robux on the wrong side of the rule. Riot says it will not help with cards bought from non-authorized sellers ([Riot Support](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/riot/events/prepaid-gift-cards)). Each banned buyer becomes a dispute against you.

Region locks have closed the old shortcut of buying cheap foreign cards. PlayStation codes work only in the store region fixed when the account was made ([PlayStation Support](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/store/ps-store-voucher-code-problems/)); V-Bucks and Riot cards are region-locked ([Epic](https://www.epicgames.com/help/fortnite-battle-royale-c-202300000001636/a202300000010238); [Riot Support](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/riot/events/prepaid-gift-cards)); Steam moved Turkey and Argentina to dollar pricing in November 2023 ([SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/blog/steam-turkey-argentina-usd)); Turkish shops sell 660 UC for about $9.31–$9.40, almost the dollar price ([Durmaplay](https://www.durmaplay.com/en/pubg-mobile-660-uc-global); [Hipopotamya](https://www.hipopotamya.com/en/category/pubg-mobile); [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/turkey/currency/news/597666)); and Moonton now gives non-Brazilian accounts 10% fewer diamonds when paying in Brazilian reais ([Smile.one Brazil](https://www.smile.one/merchant/mobilelegends)). Eldorado requires gift cards to "match the region the buyer ordered" ([Gift card guidelines](https://support.eldorado.gg/en/articles/11703747-gift-card-seller-guidelines)). Roblox cards have worked worldwide since March 2024 ([Dexerto](https://dexerto.pages.dev/posts/roblox-removes-region-locked-gift-cards-fans-are-delighted)), but they already sell under face on Eldorado.

From this evidence comes a working warning list — a judgment, not an official standard. Treat as suspect any supplier selling same-region Steam, PlayStation, Xbox or Roblox cards more than 10–15% under face, mobile currency more than 20–30% under official, or Robux anywhere near the $3.80 developer cash-out rate. Refuse any Telegram contact or unofficial "panel": one EZ PIN applicant was offered used or test codes at **$20,000 per 100,000 codes** by someone posing as staff ([Trustpilot EZ PIN](https://dk.trustpilot.com/review/ezpin.com)).

## A small capped test with stop rules answers the rest

Public data cannot name the cheapest supplier; a short, cheap test can. First, open the free or low-cost accounts: SEAGM's API sandbox, a MooGold partner account and a GamesDrop store, plus requests to Lapakgaming for Master-tier prices and to EZ PIN and LikeCard for reseller price sheets ([SEAGM API](https://doc.openapi.seagm.io); [MooGold API](https://doc.moogold.com/python-samplecode.txt); [GamesDrop](https://gamesdrop.io/en/docs/partner-api)). Start FazerCards' five-day trial last, so all the price lists can be compared in the same window. Price only the products that can work — PUBG Mobile 660 UC (top-up below $8.08, code below $7.93), Steam $50 and $100 US, PlayStation $20 US, and the Honkai: Star Rail pass once you have checked its Eldorado price — and test only suppliers that beat the 10%-margin limits above. At the same time, check whether the boosting account meets the 250-sale, 98% bar and apply for top-up access, apply for gift-card approval, and ask FazerCards in writing whether Eldorado resale is allowed. SEAGM, MooGold, GamesDrop and FazerCards all take orders by API, so your existing Eldorado bot can be extended to fulfil orders; GamesDrop checks a Player ID before charging ([GamesDrop](https://gamesdrop.io/en/docs/partner-api)), and SEAGM tells partners to trust its status query over its webhooks ([SEAGM API](https://doc.openapi.seagm.io)).

Then sell a live batch of 10–20 units per product that passes. Keep supplier balances to a few days of expected sales, spread across two or three suppliers. Save the supplier invoice and order time for every code, because Eldorado accepts only up to four images or a short video of 10 MB as dispute evidence ([Dispute process](https://intercom.help/eldoradogg/en/articles/8409016-order-dispute-process)). Eldorado top-up sellers typically promise delivery in 1–20 minutes ([Eldorado MLBB](https://www.eldorado.gg/mobile-legends-diamonds/t/55)), so a supplier that is often slower will cost you disputes. Judge the batch on these numbers; the 10% go line exists because of the bad-code arithmetic above:

| Measure | Go | Stop |
|---|---|---|
| Net margin per unit after Eldorado fee and withdrawal | 10% of the sale price or more | Under 5% |
| Supplier orders delivered | 98% or more | Any failed order not refunded automatically |
| Median delivery time | Under 5 minutes | Often over 20 minutes |
| Invalid or already-used codes | None | One bad code the supplier will not replace |
| Disputes lost on Eldorado | None | Any loss: pause and fix your evidence process |

Skip the rest. Robux breaks Roblox's rules, and its likely 15% fee and 7-day hold make it the worst-paid category; Fortnite V-Bucks top-ups need the buyer's login; and Free Fire, Mobile Legends, Genshin and Valorant top-ups sell below every legitimate source found.

## Conclusion

The search for the cheapest supplier starts from the wrong place. On Eldorado, the price of most top-ups is set by sellers whose supply a new reseller cannot reach, or should not: Robux that only makes sense as developer earnings sold outside Roblox, V-Bucks delivered through the buyer's login, Mobile Legends passes at Brazilian prices. A wholesaler cheap enough to match them is, by the same arithmetic, the one most likely to be selling stolen or rule-breaking stock, so in this market a low price is a warning sign rather than an advantage. What remains for clean supply is a thin layer — large Steam and PlayStation cards, and PUBG UC only if a reseller tier really delivers close to 19% off — and that layer is small by design, because the margin sits exactly where buyers are few.

That makes the bet unbalanced. The upside is cents to about a dollar per unit; the downside is a permanent ban on the only seller account Eldorado allows you, which is also the account your boosting business depends on. Your real assets here are not any supplier but that account's sales history, its feedback score and the API bot you have already built. Run the capped test on top of them, and if no product clears the 10% bar on real price sheets, stop — chasing a deeper discount moves you straight toward the stock that gets accounts closed.
